Products

What We Build

Two tools for two audiences. Each one takes a system most people weren't taught to navigate and makes it legible.

Decode38

VA Disability Claims

The VA claims process shouldn't require an attorney. Decode38 helps veterans understand their rating, prepare for exams, and file with confidence.

  • Estimate your VA disability rating before filing
  • Translate symptoms into VA language
  • Prepare for C&P exams with guided walkthroughs
  • AI-assisted personal statements
  • 26 service-connected conditions covered
  • Free to use, donation supported

Why We Built This

Axiom 38 was founded by a disabled veteran who learned that the systems meant to serve people — the VA, schools, universities — only work for those who already know the rules.

Every veteran deserves to understand their rating and file their claim without hiring someone to translate the VA's language. Every student deserves to understand the reasoning behind every equation, not just memorize it.

We build the tools that close that gap.

Pete Kalogiros, Founder
